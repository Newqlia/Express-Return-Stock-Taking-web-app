Quick Operating Guide

A practical checklist for stock takers, cashiers and business managers.

01

Owner or Manager: Set Up

  1. Select the correct business in the top bar. Use Add Business to create a separate business workspace.
  2. Add each product once, with its selling price and purchase price.
  3. For the first stock-taking day, enter the physical opening quantity for each product under Opening Stock.
  4. Check the selected business date before recording or reviewing activity.
02

Stock Taker: Count Stock

  1. Confirm the business and date at the top of the app.
  2. Open Daily Stock. Check the opening quantity and enter any stock additions for each product.
  3. Count the physical closing stock on the shelf and enter it in Closing.
  4. Select Save Stock on every product row. A row is counted only after its daily stock has been saved.
  5. Review Physical Sold and Variance. Physical Sold is opening stock plus additions minus closing stock.
03

Cashier: Record Sales

  1. Open Sales & Receipts and select the products and quantities sold.
  2. Choose the payment method and complete the sale to create its receipt.
  3. Record each sale once, on the date it happened. Receipt Sold in reports comes from these saved receipts.
  4. At close, compare receipt units with Physical Sold; investigate any difference rather than changing figures to force a match.
04

Expense or Purchase Recorder

  1. Record operating costs under Expenses, using the correct business date.
  2. Record new stock bought under Purchases, including quantity and amount paid.
  3. Purchases and expenses are used when calculating expected reconciliation money. Enter them once and on the correct date.
05

Manager or Accountant: Reconcile

  1. Confirm sales, purchases and expenses for the business date have been entered before reconciling.
  2. Count the actual cash and till balance, then enter each amount under Reconciliation.
  3. Select Save Reconciliation. Expected Money is receipt sales minus recorded purchases and expenses.
  4. Actual Money is cash at hand plus till. Difference is actual money minus expected money.
  5. BALANCED means the figures match. Investigate SHORTAGE or SURPLUS against receipts and recorded costs.
06

Read Daily and Monthly Reports

  • Daily Report compares physical stock movement with receipt sales for one date.
  • Monthly Business Summary groups recorded activity by day and summarizes sales, gross profit, expenses and reconciliation.
  • Products Counted shows confirmed closing counts against active products expected for the day or month.
  • Partial count means some products were saved but not all. Variance is only reported for days when all active products were counted.
  • INCOMPLETE means there are active days without complete stock counts or reconciliations. NEEDS REVIEW signals a shortage or counted-stock variance.
